# Medical Policy PA-RAD-014: Lumbar Spine MRI

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to outpatient requests for magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the
lumbar spine without contrast (CPT 72148) for adult members.

1.2 This policy does not apply to emergency department imaging or to imaging ordered as part
of an inpatient stay.

## 2. Coverage criteria

Lumbar spine MRI is considered medically necessary when all of the criteria in sections 2.1
through 2.4 are documented in the medical record.

2.1 Conservative therapy. The member has low back pain or lumbar radiculopathy that has not
improved after at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy. Conservative therapy means supervised
physical therapy, a trial of NSAIDs, or both. The record must state the duration of therapy.

2.2 Red-flag screening. The record documents that the member was screened for red-flag
symptoms and that none are present. Red-flag symptoms include bowel or bladder dysfunction,
saddle anesthesia, progressive neurological deficit, fever, unexplained weight loss, and a
history of cancer. Members with red-flag symptoms must be referred through the urgent imaging
pathway, which is outside the scope of this policy.

2.3 Neurological examination. A neurological examination of the lower extremities is
documented, including at least one of strength, reflexes, sensation, or straight-leg raise.

2.4 No recent imaging. The record shows that the member has not had a lumbar spine MRI within
the 12 months before the request date.

## 3. Required documentation

3.1 A clinical note from the ordering provider dated within 30 days of the request.

3.2 Documentation of conservative therapy with its duration, such as a physical therapy
discharge summary or a progress note.

3.3 An imaging history covering at least the previous 12 months.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 Imaging requested only for acute low back pain of less than 6 weeks without red-flag
symptoms.

4.2 Repeat imaging within 12 months without a documented change in clinical status.

## 5. Review and appeals

5.1 Requests that do not document every criterion in section 2 are denied with the reason
code of the first missing criterion: CT-DURATION (2.1), RF-SCREEN (2.2), NEURO-EXAM (2.3),
PRIOR-IMAGING (2.4).

5.2 First-level review is automated and accepts evidence from any submitted record.

5.3 A denied request may be appealed once per denial. The appeal must address the stated
reason code and cite the supporting documentation.
