# Pharmacy Policy PA-RX-310: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Weight Management

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This document sets out when Payer A covers semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly for chronic weight
management in adults. It does not cover GLP-1 drugs prescribed for diabetes alone.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 BMI means body mass index in kg/m2, measured at a clinic visit.

2.2 A weight-related comorbidity is hypertension, type 2 diabetes, dyslipidemia, or obstructive
sleep apnea, documented as a current diagnosis.

## 3. Eligibility requirements

To be eligible, the member must satisfy both requirements in this section and the safety
requirement in section 4.

3.1 Body mass index. The most recent BMI is 30 or greater, or 27 or greater with at least one
weight-related comorbidity as defined in section 2.2.

3.2 Lifestyle program. The member has taken part in a reduced-calorie diet and increased
physical activity program for at least 6 months before the request.

## 4. Safety requirements

4.1 Thyroid cancer screening. The record documents that the member has no personal or family
history of medullary thyroid carcinoma and no history of multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2.

## 5. Determinations

5.1 Requests that do not meet sections 3 and 4 are not approved. Reason codes: BMI-THRESHOLD
(3.1), LIFESTYLE-PROGRAM (3.2), MTC-SCREEN (4.1).

5.2 A denial may be appealed once with documentation that answers the reason code.
