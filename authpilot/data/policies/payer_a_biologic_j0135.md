# Medical Policy PA-RX-207: Adalimumab for Rheumatoid Arthritis

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to requests for adalimumab (HCPCS J0135) for adult members with
rheumatoid arthritis (RA).

1.2 Other indications for adalimumab are covered by separate policies.

## 2. Coverage criteria

Adalimumab is considered medically necessary for rheumatoid arthritis when all of the
criteria in sections 2.1 through 2.4 are documented in the medical record.

2.1 Confirmed diagnosis. The member has a diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis that was made or
confirmed by a rheumatologist.

2.2 Prior conventional therapy. The member has tried methotrexate for at least 3 months at a
dose of at least 15 mg weekly with an inadequate response, or methotrexate was discontinued
because of documented intolerance or toxicity.

2.3 Tuberculosis screening. A tuberculosis screening test (interferon-gamma release assay or
tuberculin skin test) with a negative result was performed within the 12 months before the
request date. Indeterminate results do not satisfy this criterion.

2.4 Disease activity. Current moderate or high disease activity is documented with a DAS28
score greater than 3.2.

## 3. Required documentation

3.1 A rheumatology consultation or progress note dated within 90 days of the request.

3.2 Methotrexate history including dose and duration, or the reason for discontinuation.

3.3 The tuberculosis screening result with its collection date.

3.4 The numeric DAS28 score. A description such as "active disease" without a score does not
meet section 2.4.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 Concurrent use of another biologic or targeted synthetic DMARD.

4.2 Active or untreated latent tuberculosis infection.

## 5. Review and appeals

5.1 Requests that do not document every criterion in section 2 are denied with the reason
code of the first missing criterion: DX-CONFIRM (2.1), MTX-TRIAL (2.2), TB-SCREEN (2.3),
DAS28-SCORE (2.4).

5.2 First-level review is automated. It accepts evidence only from the ordering provider's
consultation or progress notes (including addenda) and from laboratory reports. Evidence that
appears only in other records, such as flowsheets, is considered on appeal.

5.3 A denied request may be appealed once per denial. The appeal must address the stated
reason code and cite the supporting documentation, such as a flowsheet or laboratory report.
