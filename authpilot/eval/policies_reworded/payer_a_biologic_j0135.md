# Drug Guideline PA-RX-207: Adalimumab in Rheumatoid Arthritis

## 1. Overview

1.1 This guideline covers adalimumab (HCPCS J0135) for adult members with rheumatoid arthritis.
Other uses of the drug follow separate guidelines.

## 2. Terms used

2.1 Conventional therapy means methotrexate.

2.2 DAS28 is the 28-joint Disease Activity Score.

## 3. Criteria for coverage

Every item below must be documented.

3.1 Disease activity. A current DAS28 greater than 3.2, showing moderate or high activity, is
recorded as a number.

3.2 Tuberculosis test. A negative interferon-gamma release assay or tuberculin skin test was done
within the 12 months before the request date. Indeterminate results do not count.

3.3 Diagnosis by a specialist. A rheumatologist made or confirmed the diagnosis of rheumatoid
arthritis.

3.4 Methotrexate trial. Methotrexate was taken for at least 3 months at 15 mg or more per week
without enough benefit, or it was stopped because of intolerance or toxicity.

## 4. Paperwork to include

4.1 A rheumatology note from the last 90 days.

4.2 The methotrexate dose and duration, or why it was stopped.

4.3 The tuberculosis result and the date it was collected.

4.4 The DAS28 value itself; words such as "active disease" are not enough.

## 5. Not covered

5.1 Use together with another biologic or targeted synthetic DMARD.

## 6. Determinations and appeals

6.1 A request missing any item in section 3 is denied with the code of the first missing item:
DAS28-SCORE (3.1), TB-SCREEN (3.2), DX-CONFIRM (3.3), MTX-TRIAL (3.4).

6.2 First-level review is automated. It accepts evidence only from the ordering provider's
consultation or progress notes (including addenda) and from laboratory reports. Evidence that
appears only in other records, such as flowsheets, is considered on appeal.

6.3 One appeal is allowed per denial. It must answer the reason code and point to the records.
