# Imaging Guideline PA-RAD-014: MRI of the Lower Back

## 1. Overview

1.1 Payer A pays for an outpatient MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast (CPT 72148) for adult
members only when the documentation described below is present. Imaging in the emergency
department or during a hospital stay is handled under other rules.

## 2. Terms used

2.1 Conservative care means physical therapy under supervision, anti-inflammatory medication
(NSAIDs), or a combination of the two.

2.2 Red-flag symptoms are bowel or bladder dysfunction, saddle anesthesia, a worsening
neurological deficit, fever, unexplained weight loss, or a history of cancer.

## 3. Criteria for coverage

All four items below must be documented.

3.1 Neurological findings. The chart contains an examination of the nerves of the legs that
covers at least one of strength, reflexes, sensation, or straight-leg raise.

3.2 Failed conservative care. Back pain or radicular symptoms continue after at least 6 weeks of
conservative care, and the length of that care is written in the record.

3.3 Imaging history. The record shows that the member has not had a lumbar spine MRI within the
12 months before the request date.

3.4 Screening for red flags. The member was asked about red-flag symptoms and the record states
that none are present.

## 4. Paperwork to include

4.1 The ordering clinician's most recent note, no older than 30 days.

4.2 Proof of conservative care and how long it lasted.

4.3 A list of earlier imaging for at least the past year.

## 5. Not covered

5.1 MRI for new back pain of less than 6 weeks without red-flag symptoms.

## 6. Determinations and appeals

6.1 A request missing any item in section 3 is denied with the code of the first missing item:
NEURO-EXAM (3.1), CT-DURATION (3.2), PRIOR-IMAGING (3.3), RF-SCREEN (3.4).

6.2 First-level review is automated and accepts evidence from any submitted record.

6.3 One appeal is allowed per denial. It must answer the reason code and point to the records.
